# 🏥 Daycare Healthcare Platform

A healthcare web platform developed during my internship at **Jeddah Mental Health Hospital** to support mental health patient follow-up after hospital discharge.

The project was presented at the hospital's **Innovation Exhibition**.

---

# 📖 Problem

Mental health hospitals care for a large number of patients, making it difficult for healthcare providers to perform regular follow-up calls after discharge.

As a result, changes in a patient's condition may go unnoticed until the next scheduled appointment.

---

# 💡 Solution

The Daycare Healthcare Platform was designed to simplify post-discharge patient follow-up.

Instead of relying on frequent phone calls, one designated family member completes a short daily assessment through the platform.

The assigned doctor can monitor every patient's condition from a centralized dashboard and quickly identify patients who may require medical attention.

---

# 👨‍⚕️ How It Works

## Family Member

- Completes a daily assessment.
- Answers simple evaluation questions.
- Submits the patient's condition.

## Doctor

- Monitors all assigned patients.
- Reviews daily reports.
- Receives alerts when a patient's condition becomes critical.

---

# 🚨 Smart Alert System

If the patient's daily evaluation score falls below **5 out of 9**, the platform automatically notifies the responsible doctor, allowing faster medical intervention.

---

# 🎯 Impact

- Reduces routine follow-up phone calls.
- Helps doctors prioritize critical patients.
- Improves communication with patients' families.
- Supports continuous mental healthcare after discharge.
